SYNTH / GET STARTED

From OnliYou to your wallet.

Synth is the public demo Appliance. Start with your Onli identity, then create and use your wallet on testnet.

1. Download OnliYou

Install OnliYou on your iPhone. It is where you establish your Onli identity and respond to authentication and approval requests.

Download OnliYou from the App Store →

2. Get an invitation to Synth

You need an invitation from Synth, the demo Appliance. Accept your invitation and complete the OnliYou setup it requires. Installing OnliYou alone does not give you membership in Synth.

3. Log in with your Onli ID

Once your Synth membership is active, enter your Onli ID in the connected Synth application and approve its authentication request in OnliYou. Synth should then register your membership and provision your wallet. If setup is still pending, continue the same setup rather than starting another registration.

4. Open your wallet

After provisioning completes, open your wallet. Confirm that the wallet belongs to your signed-in identity and shows the test network before using it.

5. Send on testnet

Fund the wallet with test tokens and test ETH for fees. Choose the recipient, enter an amount and review the exact network and fee. Approve the requested behavior and confirm the transfer. Follow its activity until transaction evidence establishes the outcome. Test tokens are not real funds.
